## Type

- **Typeface:** **Neue Haas Unica W1G** (commercial; a Monotype family), named on m03. Weight shown: **Regular**
  ("Optical size: 600 pt" is the specimen size). Everything (headlines, body, labels, numerals, the word-mark) is
  this one grotesk; no second face appears. The word-mark looks set in a heavier cut (Medium) with tight spacing.
- **Scale as seen** (relative): word-mark on billboards and box lids at the largest size; poster headlines about 5%
  of the sheet width with leading about 1.12; website hero about 3% of the viewport width (about 44 px at 1440),
  leading about 1.12; body about 14 px at 1440, leading about 1.3; micro text (addresses, footers) about 1.1% of a
  sheet width; oversized numerals in data ("15.420 MW", "68%") about 3x the headline. Suggested site scale: 11, 13,
  14, 16, 20, 28, 44, 64, 96, 160 px.
- **Letter-spacing:** body and headlines at the face's default (headlines very slightly tight, about -0.01em); the
  word-mark very tight (about -0.035em) so letters touch ("Be", "es", "st") with a narrow word space (about 0.2 of
  the cap height between "t" and "G").
- **Case and punctuation habits:** sentence case everywhere; capitals only in acronyms and units ("IDAA", "BGS",
  "ID", "MW", "Q1"). Headlines broken into two or three short lines and ended with a full stop. Square brackets as a
  device: "[+34]", "[Madrid]", "[24 – 25]", "[€0.9]". Middle dots as separators: "Análisis · Criterio ·
  Validación", "Acceso a red · Conexión". Superscript "%" beside large numerals. "Nº" for numbers.
- **Two-tone line:** a sentence starts in light grey-white and continues in Dark Blue on Active Blue grounds
  ("Convertimos complejidad" / "en avance. Dando estructura…", "No se trata de producir más." / "Se trata de poder
  conectar.", "Generar energía…" / "Hacer viable…").
- Text colours: Dark Blue on light, Active Blue for body and labels on light, light grey-white on Dark Blue and
  Active Blue.

## Shapes and components

- **Guide lines and nodes (the system's signature):** every layout is drawn on visible hairlines (about 1 px at
  1440; 0.1% of a poster width) that run edge to edge, with small solid squares (about 4 to 5x the line weight, for
  example 6 px) at their crossings and at the corners of the live area. Lines are Dark Blue on light, Active Blue
  or light grey on dark. Layouts are cells between these lines.
- **Grid:** posters use a 6-column grid with narrow gutters, margins about 6.7% of the width, and a baseline grid
  of hairlines (m10). Sheets are 3:4.
- **Buttons (website):** square corners (radius 0), about 36 px tall at 1440, label 12 px Dark Blue, then an Active
  Blue crosshair (four short bars around an empty centre, about 16 px). Primary: pale fill (about #EEF2FF) on Light
  Blue. Secondary: 1 px light outline, no fill. CTA in the nav: a full-height Dark Blue cell, Active Blue label
  "Únete" plus a white crosshair.
- **Nav:** one row of ruled cells; mark in the first cell; links centred in their own cells; nodes on the bottom
  rule under cell borders.
- **Stat tiles:** ruled cells with a pixel icon, a label and a value, Active Blue type; one tile highlighted with a
  periwinkle fill.
- **Chips:** fully rounded pills (radius = height / 2) with a year label; inactive pale grey-blue, active Active
  Blue with white text. The only rounded UI element.
- **KPI cells:** ruled cells with nodes, small Dark Blue label over a large Active Blue numeral.
- **Slider controls:** an Active Blue pill (about 3 dot-widths long) plus white dots; a white circular pause button.
- **Cards and print:** square-cornered sheets; paper with fine grain; blind emboss of the mark; painted card edges in
  Active Blue; ID holder with radius about 9% of its width.
- **Charts:** area chart with an Active Blue fill at about 45% opacity broken into soft vertical bands, grey
  horizontal gridlines, small axis labels "x.000 MW", square nodes on the baseline at Q1 to Q4, a vertical marker
  line at the peak. Treemap of square-cornered blocks in Dark Blue, Active Blue and a lighter Active Blue with
  oversized numerals bottom right. Node grid: square cells with hairline outlines, filled in two strengths of Active
  Blue (full and about 75%), clustered, with some quarter-size cells.
- **Icons:** pixel icons on a 32 x 32 grid; stroke 1 unit (1/32 of the frame), square ends, built from orthogonal
  bars; where bars would meet at a corner the corner pixel is left out (stepped corners); diagonals and arcs keep
  the 1-unit width (turbine). The four shown: Fotovoltaico, Eólico, Almacenamiento, Data Centers. Turbine (m14):
  centred on (16,16); a hub ring of diameter 3 units with a 1 x 1 square inside; three blades at 120°, each two
  converging bars with a 1 x 1 square at the tip (top tip at y = 5.5); a broken outer ring of radius 7 (1 unit
  thick) cut where the blades pass; overall about 18.7 x 17.5 units.
- **Device frames:** a desktop browser with tab and address bar (m16), a laptop (m13), phones with a pill-shaped
  camera cut-out and status bar "9:41" (m17). Draw them neutral (see "Other companies' names").
- **Tables:** none published. **Inputs:** none published. **Footer:** posters use a three-cell ruled footer
  ("bestgrid.com" | mark | "bestgrid.com"); the website footer is not shown.

## Gradients, textures and backgrounds

- **No gradient is published.** The brand fields are flat: Dark Blue, Active Blue, Light Blue, periwinkle.
- Photography carries the only gradients: fog (peach #D9B8A6 to plum #4A2E2E, top-left light), sunset
  (orange #E0763A under Dark Blue sky), light trails (cyan #5FD8FF lines). Extensions may use these as SVG linear
  and radial gradients standing in for photos.
- **Textures:** fine paper grain on every printed surface (an feTurbulence noise at baseFrequency about 0.9, 2
  octaves, mixed at 4 to 8% opacity); uncoated board grain on the box (coarser, about 0.6); metallic bubble film on
  the mailer (a hexagon pattern with specular highlights).
- **Baseline field:** horizontal hairlines every 0.55% of the sheet height, alternating Active Blue and grey,
  interrupted by white column gutters (m10). Redraw as an SVG pattern.
- **Photo stand-ins in the system:** flat Dark Blue rectangles at about 75% opacity over the grid (m10).
- **Presentation grounds:** periwinkle for objects, black for posters and screens, Dark Blue for icon slides.

## Logo geometry

**Word-mark:** "Best Grid" in the brand grotesk, heavier cut (about Medium), one line, letters touching, narrow word
space. Width : cap height = about 5.54 : 1; "Best" and "Grid" about equal widths (740 : 711); the gap "t"–"G" =
0.2 cap height. Trace from the face (outlined), never retype.

**Mark** (from `logo_zoom.png`, measured on m21 at 1013 px): a four-pointed star whose body is a lattice, like a
transmission tower or a grid seen in perspective. On a 100 x 100 box (y down), fill-rule evenodd:
- **Outline:** tips toward the four corners, each tip cut by a 45° chamfer (x + y = 3.4 at the top-left tip, so

## Motion personality

Engineered, exact and calm: lines extend along the guides first, nodes snap in at the crossings, then content fills
the cells; nothing bounces. The only published motion (m10) is a hard-cut cycle at 100 ms per frame.
Tokens: `--dur-snap` 100 ms (hard cut, one m10 frame), `--dur-xs` 160 ms (nodes, chips), `--dur-s` 240 ms (hover,
press), `--dur-m` 420 ms (cell fills, panels), `--dur-l` 700 ms (page enter), `--dur-draw` 1200 ms (guide lines
and the mark's lattice drawing on). Easing: `--ease-out` cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) for entering,
`--ease-move` cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for moves, `--ease-line` cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.2, 1) for line
draws, `steps(8, end)` at 100 ms for slideshow cycles. Stagger 40 ms across cells. No overshoot.
