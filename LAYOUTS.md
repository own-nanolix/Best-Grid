# LAYOUTS: one line per page, one line per mockup

Direction for this project (from `rule/10-best-grid.md`, section 2b):

- **Mockup staging:** catalogue plate: object on one side, spec callouts with leader lines and dimensions on the other.
- **Mockup backdrop:** a dark stage with one soft spotlight (`StageG`; the spot sets the one light direction).
- **Brand pages lean on:** spec-sheet tables with oversized numerals (`SpecTable`, `Numeral`, `.spec`), a different
  grid on every page.
- **Dashboard option A is:** a command bar over a dense data table; B to E are four other structures.
- **Landing pages lean on:** alternating split rows of text and artwork (`SplitRow`), seven different structures.

Rules: where the case study shows a layout, the case study wins (marked "as mNN"). Every page is drawn on the
brand's own guide lines: hairlines that run edge to edge with square nodes at the crossings. Nothing repeats: no
two lines below may be built the same way. Builders follow their lines; reviewers check against them.

## Mockups (arrangement and camera)

Every mockup is one SVG on the dark stage (`StageG`) with one soft spotlight; the spot position sets the light;
shadows fall away from it; every object stands on a contact shadow (`ShadowG`). The object sits on one side of the
plate, the callouts (`CalloutG`), dimensions (`DimensionG`) and the spec column (`SpecColumnG`) on the other. The
side, the split, the camera and the spot change every time.
